# LibriSpeech clean canary fixtures

These three utterances are from the **LibriSpeech ASR corpus, clean validation
split**, distributed under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Source corpus: <https://www.openslr.org/12>

The filenames are LibriSpeech utterance IDs. The corresponding speaker,
chapter, authoritative transcript, and SHA-256 are recorded in
[`../manifest-v2.json`](../manifest-v2.json). The WAV files are normalized to
uncompressed mono 16 kHz signed 16-bit PCM for the SayAll provider canary.

LibriSpeech citation:

> Vassil Panayotov, Guoguo Chen, Daniel Povey and Sanjeev Khudanpur,
> “LibriSpeech: an ASR corpus based on public domain audio books,” ICASSP 2015.
