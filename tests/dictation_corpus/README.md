# SayAll in-domain dictation corpus

This directory defines the recording and acceptance policy for the primary
SayAll quality gate. The checked-in LibriSpeech corpora remain provider canary
and stress evidence; they are not substitutes for this corpus.

## Recording target

Record at least **2,000 unique verbatim words** across at least two consenting
speakers; three or more remains preferable for broader coverage. A word is
counted once per clean source recording; generated 10 dB and 5 dB noise variants
do not increase corpus size.

For the initial two-speaker corpus, use `recording-scripts-v1.json`: one female
speaker records every Maya entry and one male speaker records every Daniel entry,
with each entry saved as a separate file. The pack contains 2,399 normalized
verbatim words, leaving room for an unusable take while retaining the 2,000-word
minimum. Read intentional fillers and repetitions exactly. Do not perform both
characters in one file or have one person imitate both speakers.

`scenarios-v1.json` remains available for later spontaneous additions. Do not
include private names, credentials, customer information, or other sensitive
content.

For every recording, create:

- `verbatim_reference`: exactly what was spoken, including fillers and repeats;
- `clean_reference`: the desired deterministic Clean output;
- `protected_terms`: names, numbers, dates, paths, commands, and technical terms
  that Clean must not corrupt or remove.

Audio must be consented, redistributable, mono 16 kHz signed-16 PCM WAV. Record
clean source audio first; deterministic office-noise variants are generated by
the benchmark and retain the same references.

## Validation

Store the completed benchmark manifest beside its audio and run:

```sh
python3 tests/dictation_corpus/validate.py path/to/manifest.json
```

The validator rejects fewer than 2,000 normalized verbatim words, fewer than
two speakers, missing scenario categories, unsafe paths, noncanonical audio,
hash mismatches, missing dual references, and unprotected Clean references.

The live benchmark supports both product modes:

```sh
python3 tests/deepgram_benchmark/benchmark.py \
  --manifest path/to/manifest.json \
  --processing-profiles verbatim,clean --mode both --runs 3
```

Clean is the primary profile for release decisions. Verbatim remains the raw
STT diagnostic baseline; the two profiles are scored against different human
references and must never be compared to one shared transcript.
